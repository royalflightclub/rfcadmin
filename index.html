Contracts & ticket allowances

Automatic one-year contracts start on the joining-date anniversary and end the day before the next anniversary. This is a calculated schedule, not proof of a signed renewal. Switch off automatic renewal for manual dates. For 29 February joins, anniversaries are treated as 28 February in non-leap years.

Ticket allowance frequency: joining before 2020 → yearly; joining in 2020 or later → every two years. Ticket entitlement is automatic on the joining-date anniversary at the relevant contract renewal: every renewal for pre-2020 joins, every second renewal for 2020 onwards. The latest entitlement at setup is tracked; earlier payment history has not been imported. Each entitlement has a Received / Not yet received status. Historical entries without a status show Not recorded for admin review. Receiving an allowance does not hide it; the next eligible renewal opens a new entitlement. Previous payment records are retained as Received.

Vacation balances

Entitlement is annual and the leave year starts on the joining-date anniversary. Vacation start/end dates are inclusive calendar days. Set a verified starting balance once. From that date, unused leave accumulates without a yearly reset, and the staff-specific annual entitlement is added on each joining-date anniversary. Approved/completed leave is deducted and future approved leave is reserved. Earlier unrecorded leave is not guessed. A later verified balance acts as a correction/checkpoint, not an extra annual grant. Annual addition history is retained.

Protect your records

Staff data is saved in this browser on this device. It is not shared with other computers or uploaded to GitHub. Anyone with access to this browser profile can view it. Browser storage and downloaded backups are not encrypted by this app.

No document numbers or scanned copies are needed. Use a protected office computer and store backups in an approved private location.
